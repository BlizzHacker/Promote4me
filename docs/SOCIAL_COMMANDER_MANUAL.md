# Promote4.me Social Media Commander - Complete Manual

## Overview
Social Media Commander modernizes what classic automation suites (FollowLiker, MassPlanner, Jarvee) did, but replaces brittle UI scrapers with your own AI agents (Hermes, Qwen, Deepseek, Claude, Codex, Gemini, Antigravity).

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Multi-Platform Account Vault
- Connect Reddit, Facebook Pages/Groups, Instagram, Twitter/X, Discord, YouTube, TikTok, Bluesky, Telegram, Threads.
- Configure dedicated proxies (`http://user:pass@host:port`) per account to prevent IP linking and bot flagging.
- Set independent daily quotas for posts, comments, direct messages, likes, and follows.

### 2. Post Publishing & Smart Scheduling
- **Immediate Post**: Broadcast to one or multiple networks simultaneously.
- **Natural Human Scheduling**: Automatically calculates randomized time slots (30-90 minutes apart) within active hours (09:00 - 21:00) with 1-15 minute jitter to mimic authentic human behavior.
- **Calendar Management**: Query, inspect, reschedule, or cancel queued posts.

### 3. Comment Management & Auto-Reply
- Monitor incoming comments across connected platforms.
- Sentiment analysis and categorization (questions, leads, feedback).
- Post direct replies or configure automated keyword rules:
  - Example: When a comment contains "pricing" or "how to join", automatically send a friendly response or notify the team.

### 4. Direct Message Auto-Responders
- Monitor inbound DMs.
- Dispatch instant welcome greetings or AI-crafted responses.

### 5. FollowLiker / MassPlanner Growth Campaigns
- **Follow / Unfollow**: Target followers of competitors or niche accounts with safe daily caps (e.g., max 50 follows/day) and warm-up pacing.
- **Like / Upvote Storms**: Engage with posts in target subreddits or hashtags.
- **Working Hours & Safety Limits**: Built-in cooling periods, proxy rotation, and daily caps prevent shadowbans.

### 6. Full Audit Trail
- Every action, timestamp, platform response ID, and error is recorded in `social_activity_log`.
