Privacy Policy for Promote4.me Social Media Commander

Effective Date: November 2026

Promote4.me ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes how our Social Media Commander MCP and Custom Actions interact with user data.

1. Data Collection & Usage

The Social Media Commander only accesses and processes social media data (posts, comments, messages, account identifiers) that you explicitly configure or authorize through your connected social accounts.

2. Credentials & Security

Social platform credentials, access tokens, and cookies are stored securely within your tenant database. We do not sell, share, or broker your personal information or credentials to third parties.

3. Third-Party Platforms

When you publish posts, reply to comments, or schedule campaigns, interactions occur directly with the respective third-party platforms (Reddit, Meta/Facebook/Instagram, X/Twitter, Discord, etc.) in accordance with their respective APIs and terms.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@promote4.me.
